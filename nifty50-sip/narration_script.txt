What if you invested just ten thousand rupees every year in the Nifty 50? Let's find out.

The year is 2006. You put in your first ten thousand. The market surges nearly 40 percent, growing it to almost fourteen thousand. 2007 is even better. A massive 55 percent rally pushes your portfolio past 37 thousand. Then 2008 hits. The Global Financial Crisis wipes out over 50 percent. Your thirty thousand invested? Now just twenty-two thousand. But you don't stop.

2009 brings an incredible 76 percent bounce. Your portfolio jumps to 57 thousand. By 2014, steady growth takes you past one lakh sixty-six thousand, with just ninety thousand invested.

This is the power of compounding. Even through the COVID crash of 2020, your portfolio keeps climbing. By 2021, you've crossed four lakh seventy thousand.

So let's break it down. You invested a total of two lakh rupees. Your portfolio grew to seven lakh sixty-one thousand. That's five lakh sixty-one thousand in profit. Now think about inflation. In 2006, a movie ticket was fifty rupees. Petrol was forty-seven a litre. Gold was eighty-six hundred for ten grams. Today, movie tickets cost three-fifty. Petrol crosses a hundred. Gold is over ninety thousand. Your money needs to grow just to keep up, and the Nifty 50 delivered.

After twenty years, two lakh became seven lakh sixty-one thousand. A 3.8 times multiplier. Even after inflation, you beat every savings account and fixed deposit.

Start early. Stay consistent. Let time do the heavy lifting.
