# Finance Video Projects

Short-form finance explainer videos built with [HyperFrames](https://hyperframes.dev) for Instagram Reels and YouTube.

## Projects

### [Nifty 50 SIP](/nifty50-sip)
What happens when you invest INR 10,000/year in Nifty 50 from 2006-2025? A data-driven explainer showing the power of SIP investing through market crashes and recoveries.

### [Jensen Huang / NVIDIA](/jensen-nvidia)
Jensen Huang's resignation from NVIDIA - the scandal, stock crash, market impact, analyst predictions, and what it means for traders. Dark scandal-themed opener with Vox-style data analysis.

## Tech Stack
- **HyperFrames** - HTML-to-video rendering
- **GSAP** - Animation engine
- **Kokoro TTS** - AI voiceover
- **ffmpeg** - Video processing and vertical format conversion
